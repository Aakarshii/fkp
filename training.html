What we do

Training

We believe in the autonomy of every health worker cadre.

01Approach

Teaching health workers to reason like clinicians, with AI.

To achieve this, we train non-physicians in the use of LLMs. We also teach health workers how clinicians think. Then we integrate both these aspects to help health workers use LLMs to think, reason and make decisions like clinicians.

02Programme

12 nurses trained across 3 sites.

These nurses are now enrolling patients for our study. The training has two phases.

3-week intensive training

Phase one of the programme.

Ongoing supportive supervision

Phase two of the programme.

03Work with us

Train your health workers.

Please contact us to train health workers in the use of AI, or to upgrade your clinic or hospital to use AI effectively.
