What we do

Implementation Research

Evidence generation on whether AI-enabled nurses can safely and effectively deliver primary care.

Currently enrolling patients
01Pilot study

Nurse + AI Pilot Study

Sites
  • Birbhum, West Bengal
  • Purulia, West Bengal
Study design
Randomized Crossover Design
Sample size
672
Objective
Safety and efficacy of AI-enabled nurse compared to physicians
Common problems in primary care clinics
  • Diabetes Mellitus
  • Hypertension
  • Breathlessness
  • Fever
  • Musculoskeletal Pain
02Evidence

The study will generate evidence on

  1. Quality of clinical consultation when nurses are enabled with AI
  2. Qualitative data on user (nurse) experience
  3. Qualitative data on client (patient) experience
  4. Survey of client experience
03Support

Transforming rural healthcare

We won a grant to fund our first research project and are targeting two more grants in 6–12 months. We are raising funds to continue developing our mobile app and explore feasibility in new places until we win these grants.
