Transforming rural healthcare

We envision a world where advanced information technology can improve the health of people in remote rural areas of LMICs.

01The campaign

Why we are raising funds.

We have developed a mobile-based app for frontline healthcare workers to assist them with triage and initial treatment of patients.

We won a grant to fund our first research project and are targeting two more grants in 6–12 months.

We are raising funds to continue developing our mobile app and explore feasibility in new places until we win these grants.
